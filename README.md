# Accident-Forecasting-and-Predictive-Insurance-Claims-Modeling
Accidents occur worldwide due to various factors such as negligent driving and climatic conditions. You are tasked with using data to gain actionable insights from the Car Insurance Claims dataset. Your primary objective is to explore the dataset, identify patterns, and present your findings through an interactive and visually appealing storyboard.
